Writing tips

Write maths between dollar signs: $x^2 + 3x - 4 = 0$, $\frac{3}{4}$, $\sqrt{2}$, $30^\circ$, $3\,\text{m s}^{-1}$.

Text copied from PDFs loses formatting: x2 may mean $x^2$, and fractions may come out on two lines. Fix them here and check the preview.

Copy past-paper wording exactly. Describe diagrams in words in the question, and add the picture later on the site.

A good solution starts from what is given, names the rule, shows each step and says why the tempting wrong answer is wrong.
