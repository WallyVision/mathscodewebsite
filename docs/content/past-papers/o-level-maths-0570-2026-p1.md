## Q1 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Subtracting 17 from 30 gives

**Options**

- A. $13$
- B. $47$
- C. $3$
- D. $-13$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

"Subtract 17 from 30" means start with 30 and take away 17:

$$
30 - 17 = 13
$$

**Answer: A**

Option D comes from working out $17 - 30$, which is the wrong way round. Option B adds the numbers.

## Q2 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

The prime factors of 12 are

**Options**

- A. $1, 2, 3, 4$
- B. $2, 3, 4$
- C. $2, 3, 6$
- D. $2, 3$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

Write 12 as a product of primes:

$$
12 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 = 2^2 \times 3
$$

The different prime numbers that appear are 2 and 3.

**Answer: D**

1 is not a prime number, and 4 and 6 are factors of 12 but they are not prime ($4 = 2 \times 2$, $6 = 2 \times 3$).

## Q3 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

The absolute value of $-3 + 7$ is

**Options**

- A. $-4$
- B. $4$
- C. $10$
- D. $-10$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

First work out the inside: $-3 + 7 = 4$.

The absolute value is the size of a number without its sign, so it is never negative:

$$
|-3 + 7| = |4| = 4
$$

**Answer: B**

Options A and D are negative, so they cannot be absolute values. Option C works out $3 + 7$ instead.

## Q4 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

A woman spent $\frac{1}{4}$ of her salary on rents and $\frac{1}{5}$ on food. The fraction spent on both items is

**Options**

- A. $\frac{1}{20}$
- B. $\frac{2}{9}$
- C. $\frac{1}{9}$
- D. $\frac{9}{20}$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

Add the two fractions, using the common denominator 20:

$$
\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{5} = \frac{5}{20} + \frac{4}{20} = \frac{9}{20}
$$

**Answer: D**

Option A multiplies the fractions. Option B adds the tops and the bottoms separately ($\frac{1+1}{4+5}$), which is never how fractions are added.

## Q5 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Given that $0.0056 = 5.6 \times 10^m$, the value of $m$ is

**Options**

- A. $-2$
- B. $4$
- C. $-3$
- D. $3$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

To turn $5.6$ into $0.0056$, the decimal point moves **3 places to the left**, which is dividing by $1000 = 10^3$:

$$
0.0056 = \frac{5.6}{1000} = 5.6 \times 10^{-3}
$$

So $m = -3$.

**Answer: C**

A number smaller than 1 always has a negative power of 10 in standard form, so options B and D are impossible.

## Q6 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

The number $5.04678$ expressed to three significant figures gives

**Options**

- A. $5.05$
- B. $5.047$
- C. $5.0468$
- D. $5.04$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

The first three significant figures are 5, 0 and 4: $5.04|678$.

The next digit is 6, which is 5 or more, so round the 4 up to 5:

$$
5.04678 \approx 5.05 \text{ (3 s.f.)}
$$

**Answer: A**

Option D cuts the number off without rounding. Options B and C have 4 and 5 significant figures.

## Q7 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

On a certain day the temperature drops by $0.5^\circ\text{C}$ every hour. Given that at 8:00 a.m the temperature is $34.8^\circ\text{C}$, the temperature at 10:00 a.m in $^\circ\text{C}$ is

**Options**

- A. $35.8$
- B. $33.8$
- C. $34.7$
- D. $34.9$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

From 8:00 a.m to 10:00 a.m is 2 hours, so the temperature drops by

$$
2 \times 0.5 = 1.0^\circ\text{C}
$$

$$
34.8 - 1.0 = 33.8^\circ\text{C}
$$

**Answer: B**

Option A adds the drop instead of subtracting it.

## Q8 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

An article is sold at 7,200 FCFA making a profit of 20% on the cost price. The actual cost price in FCFA is

**Options**

- A. $5760$
- B. $6000$
- C. $8640$
- D. $5000$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

The profit is 20% **of the cost price**, so the selling price is $100\% + 20\% = 120\%$ of the cost price:

$$
1.2 \times \text{cost price} = 7200
$$

$$
\text{cost price} = \frac{7200}{1.2} = 6000 \text{ FCFA}
$$

Check: 20% of 6000 is 1200, and $6000 + 1200 = 7200$. ✓

**Answer: B**

Option A takes 20% off the selling price ($7200 \times 0.8$). That is the common mistake: the 20% is of the cost price, not of the selling price. Option C adds 20% to the selling price.

## Q9 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Dividing 480\$ in the ratio $1 : 2$ gives

**Options**

- A. $160 : 320$
- B. $320 : 160$
- C. $480 : 960$
- D. $960 : 480$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

The ratio $1 : 2$ has $1 + 2 = 3$ parts in total.

$$
\text{one part} = \frac{480}{3} = 160
$$

So the shares are $1 \times 160 = 160$ and $2 \times 160 = 320$, in the same order as the ratio: $160 : 320$.

Check: $160 + 320 = 480$. ✓

**Answer: A**

Option B has the shares the wrong way round. Options C and D add up to more than the 480\$ being shared.

## Q10 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

The scale of a map is $1 : 25\,000$. The distance between two towns on the map is 14cm. The actual distance between the two towns in km is

**Options**

- A. $350$
- B. $3500$
- C. $3.5$
- D. $35$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

Every 1 cm on the map is 25 000 cm on the ground.

$$
14 \times 25\,000 = 350\,000 \text{ cm}
$$

Change to kilometres: $100 \text{ cm} = 1 \text{ m}$ and $1000 \text{ m} = 1 \text{ km}$, so $1 \text{ km} = 100\,000 \text{ cm}$.

$$
\frac{350\,000}{100\,000} = 3.5 \text{ km}
$$

**Answer: C**

Option B is the distance in **metres** ($3500 \text{ m}$), not kilometres. Options A and D divide by the wrong power of 10.
