**Options**

**Correct:** A

## Q2 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

The prime factors of 12 are

**Options**

- A. $1, 2, 3, 4$
- B. $2, 3, 4$
- C. $2, 3, 6$
- D. $2, 3$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

Write 12 as a product of primes:

$$
12 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 = 2^2 \times 3
$$

The different prime numbers that appear are 2 and 3.

**Answer: D**

1 is not a prime number, and 4 and 6 are factors of 12 but they are not prime ($4 = 2 \times 2$, $6 = 2 \times 3$).

## Q3 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

The absolute value of $-3 + 7$ is

**Options**

- A. $-4$
- B. $4$
- C. $10$
- D. $-10$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

First work out the inside: $-3 + 7 = 4$.

The absolute value is the size of a number without its sign, so it is never negative:

$$
|-3 + 7| = |4| = 4
$$

**Answer: B**

Options A and D are negative, so they cannot be absolute values. Option C works out $3 + 7$ instead.

A woman spent $\frac{1}{4}$ of her salary on rents and $\frac{1}{5}$ on food. The fraction spent on both items is

**Options**

- A. $\frac{1}{20}$
- B. $\frac{2}{9}$
- C. $\frac{1}{9}$

**Correct:** D

$$
\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{5} = \frac{5}{20} + \frac{4}{20} = \frac{9}{20}
$$

**Answer: D**

Option A multiplies the fractions. Option B adds the tops and the bottoms separately ($\frac{1+1}{4+5}$), which is never how fractions are added.

## Q5 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Given that $0.0056 = 5.6 \times 10^m$, the value of $m$ is

**Options**

- A. $-2$
- B. $4$
- C. $-3$
- D. $3$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

To turn $5.6$ into $0.0056$, the decimal point moves **3 places to the left**, which is dividing by $1000 = 10^3$:

$$
0.0056 = \frac{5.6}{1000} = 5.6 \times 10^{-3}
$$

So $m = -3$.

**Answer: C**

A number smaller than 1 always has a negative power of 10 in standard form, so options B and D are impossible.

## Q8 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

An article is sold at 7,200 FCFA making a profit of 20% on the cost price. The actual cost price in FCFA is

**Options**

- A. $5760$
- B. $6000$
- C. $8640$
- D. $5000$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

The profit is 20% **of the cost price**, so the selling price is $100\% + 20\% = 120\%$ of the cost price:

$$
1.2 \times \text{cost price} = 7200
$$

$$
\text{cost price} = \frac{7200}{1.2} = 6000 \text{ FCFA}
$$

Check: 20% of 6000 is 1200, and $6000 + 1200 = 7200$. ✓

**Answer: B**

Option A takes 20% off the selling price ($7200 \times 0.8$). That is the common mistake: the 20% is of the cost price, not of the selling price. Option C adds 20% to the selling price.

## Q9 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Dividing 480\$ in the ratio $1 : 2$ gives

**Options**

- A. $160 : 320$
- B. $320 : 160$
- C. $480 : 960$
- D. $960 : 480$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

The ratio $1 : 2$ has $1 + 2 = 3$ parts in total.

$$
\text{one part} = \frac{480}{3} = 160
$$

So the shares are $1 \times 160 = 160$ and $2 \times 160 = 320$, in the same order as the ratio: $160 : 320$.

Check: $160 + 320 = 480$. ✓

**Answer: A**

Option B has the shares the wrong way round. Options C and D add up to more than the 480\$ being shared.

$$
\frac{350\,000}{100\,000} = 3.5 \text{ km}
$$

**Answer: C**

Option B is the distance in **metres** ($3500 \text{ m}$), not kilometres. Options A and D divide by the wrong power of 10.

**Stem**

The inverse of $g(x) = \frac{x + 2}{3}$ is

**Options**

- A. $2x - 3$

- C. $3x + 2$
- D. $2x - 3$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

Let $y = \frac{x + 2}{3}$ and make $x$ the subject:

$$
3y = x + 2 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x = 3y - 2

Check with $x = 4$: $g(4) = \frac{6}{3} = 2$, and $g^{-1}(2) = 3(2) - 2 = 4$. ✓

**Answer: B**

Option C undoes the steps in the wrong order: to reverse "add 2, then divide by 3", first multiply by 3, then **subtract** 2. (Options A and D are printed the same in the paper.)

## Q44 · difficulty 2

The direction in degrees of the vector $-\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j}$ in degrees is

**Options**

- A. $-45^\circ$
- B. $135^\circ$
- C. $45^\circ$
- D. $225^\circ$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

The vector goes 1 unit **left** and 1 unit **up**, so it points into the second quadrant.

Its angle with the negative $x$-axis is $\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{1}\right) = 45^\circ$. Measured anticlockwise from the positive $x$-axis, the direction is

$$
180^\circ - 45^\circ = 135^\circ
$$

**Answer: B**

Option C is the direction of $\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j}$, option D of $-\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j}$, and option A of $\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j}$. Sketching the vector shows which quadrant it is in.

The transpose $M^T$ turns the rows into columns: the first row $(2, -3)$ becomes the first column, and the second row $(1, 5)$ becomes the second column.

Option A swaps the leading diagonal as well (that step belongs to finding the inverse). Options C and D change signs, which a transpose never does.

## Q50 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

The probability of rolling a fair die once and scoring a '2' is

**Options**

- A. $\frac{1}{3}$
- B. $\frac{1}{6}$
- C. $\frac{1}{4}$
- D. $\frac{1}{5}$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

A fair die has 6 equally likely outcomes (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6), and exactly one of them is a 2:

$$
P(2) = \frac{\text{favourable outcomes}}{\text{possible outcomes}} = \frac{1}{6}
$$

**Answer: B**
