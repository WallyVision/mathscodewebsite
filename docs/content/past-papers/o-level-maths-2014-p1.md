## Q1 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

$\frac{1}{2}$ of $\left(\frac{2}{5} + \frac{3}{4}\right)$ when simplified gives

**Options**

- A. $\frac{23}{40}$
- B. $\frac{19}{20}$
- C. $\frac{10}{23}$
- D. $\frac{23}{20}$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

Brackets first:

$$
\frac{2}{5} + \frac{3}{4} = \frac{8}{20} + \frac{15}{20} = \frac{23}{20}
$$

Then "of" means multiply: $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{23}{20} = \frac{23}{40}$.

**Answer: A**

Option D forgets to take half.

## Q4 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Arranging the fractions $\frac{1}{2}, \frac{2}{3}, \frac{1}{4}$ and $\frac{1}{6}$ in descending order gives

**Options**

- A. $\frac{1}{2}, \frac{2}{3}, \frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{6}$
- B. $\frac{1}{6}, \frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{2}{3}$
- C. $\frac{2}{3}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{6}$
- D. $\frac{2}{3}, \frac{1}{6}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{4}$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

With denominator 12: $\frac{1}{2} = \frac{6}{12}$, $\frac{2}{3} = \frac{8}{12}$, $\frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{12}$, $\frac{1}{6} = \frac{2}{12}$. Descending means largest first: $\frac{8}{12}, \frac{6}{12}, \frac{3}{12}, \frac{2}{12}$.

**Answer: C**

Option B is ascending order.

The temperature of a room at 1 pm is $23^\circ\text{C}$ and it increases by $5^\circ\text{C}$ every 30 minutes. The temperature, in $^\circ\text{C}$, at 3:30 pm will be

## Q9 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

A car dealer sold a car to a lady at a discount of 10%. Given that she paid 1,800,000 FCFA, the original selling price in FCFA is

**Options**

- A. 1,600,000
- B. 1,620,000
- C. 1,980,000
- D. 2,000,000

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

After a 10% discount she paid 90% of the original price:

$$
0.9 \times P = 1\,800\,000 \quad\Rightarrow\quad P = \frac{1\,800\,000}{0.9} = 2\,000\,000 \text{ FCFA}
$$

**Answer: D**

Option C adds 10% of what she paid, but the discount is 10% of the original price.

## Q10 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

240,000 FCFA is shared in the ratio $2 : 3 : 7$. The largest share, in FCFA, is

**Options**

- A. 168,000
- B. 40,000
- C. 140,000
- D. 120,000

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

There are $2 + 3 + 7 = 12$ parts, so one part is $240\,000 \div 12 = 20\,000$. The largest share is 7 parts:

$$
7 \times 20\,000 = 140\,000 \text{ FCFA}
$$

**Answer: C**

## Q22 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

The geometric progression in the following sequences is

**Options**

- A. $1, 2, 3, 4, \ldots$
- B. $2, 4, 6, 8, \ldots$
- C. $1, 2, 7, \ldots$
- D. $3, 6, 12, \ldots$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

In a geometric progression each term is the one before times the same number. In D each term is doubled: $3 \times 2 = 6$, $6 \times 2 = 12$.

## Q27 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Given that $f : x \mapsto \frac{2x - 1}{3}$, then $f^{-1}$ expressed in a similar manner is

**Options**

- A. $f^{-1} : x \mapsto \frac{3x + 1}{2}$
- B. $f^{-1} : x \mapsto \frac{3x - 1}{2}$
- C. $f^{-1} : x \mapsto \frac{3(x - 1)}{2}$
- D. $f^{-1} : x \mapsto \frac{2x - 1}{3}$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

Let $y = \frac{2x - 1}{3}$ and make $x$ the subject:

$$
3y = 2x - 1 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x = \frac{3y + 1}{2}
$$

So $f^{-1} : x \mapsto \frac{3x + 1}{2}$.

**Answer: A**

Option B forgets that $-1$ becomes $+1$ when it moves across.

## Q28 · difficulty 2

Given that $f : x \mapsto 3x - 2$ and $g : x \mapsto 2x + 3$, then $fog$ is

**Options**

- A. $6x + 11$
- B. $6x + 7$
- C. $6x + 1$
- D. $6x + 4$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

$fog(x) = f(g(x))$: apply $g$ first, then $f$.

$$
f(2x + 3) = 3(2x + 3) - 2 = 6x + 9 - 2 = 6x + 7
$$

**Answer: B**

The order matters: $gof(x) = 2(3x - 2) + 3 = 6x - 1$ is a different function. Option A adds the 2 instead of subtracting it.

## Q32 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Given the equations of the lines $L_1 : 2x + y = 8$ and $L_2 : 6y = mx - 3$, the value of $m$ when $L_1$ and $L_2$ are perpendicular is

**Options**

- A. $12$
- B. $3$
- C. $-3$
- D. $-12$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

$L_1$: $y = -2x + 8$, gradient $-2$. $L_2$: $y = \frac{m}{6}x - \frac{1}{2}$, gradient $\frac{m}{6}$.

Perpendicular gradients multiply to $-1$:

$$
-2 \times \frac{m}{6} = -1 \quad\Rightarrow\quad m = 3
$$

**Answer: B**

Option D makes the lines parallel ($\frac{m}{6} = -2$).

## Q49 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

A number is chosen at random from the set $S = \{1, 2, 3, \ldots, 10\}$. The probability of picking a prime number is
