## Q1 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

The value of the digit 7 in $64.176$ is

**Options**

- A. $\frac{7}{10}$
- B. $\frac{7}{100}$
- C. $7$
- D. $70$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

In $64.176$ the digits after the decimal point are tenths ($1$), hundredths ($7$) and thousandths ($6$). The 7 is in the hundredths place, so its value is

$$
\frac{7}{100}
$$

**Answer: B**

Option A would be the value of a digit in the tenths place.

## Q2 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

The H.C.F of $2^3 \times 3$ and $2 \times 3^2 \times 5$ is

**Options**

- A. $2 \times 3 \times 5$
- B. $2^3 \times 3^2$
- C. $2 \times 3$
- D. $2^4 \times 3^2 \times 5$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

The H.C.F takes each prime that appears in **both** numbers, with the **lower** power:

- $2$: powers $2^3$ and $2^1$, take $2^1$;
- $3$: powers $3^1$ and $3^2$, take $3^1$;
- $5$ is not in the first number, so it is left out.

$$
\text{H.C.F} = 2 \times 3 = 6
$$

**Answer: C**

Option B uses the higher powers, which is the method for the L.C.M.

The number $\sqrt{7}$, belongs to the set of

**Options**

- A. Integers
- B. Natural Numbers
- C. Rational Numbers
- D. Irrational Numbers

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

## Q7 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Given that $0.0078$ can be written as $a \times 10^n$ where $1 \le a < 10$, then the value of $n$ is

**Options**

- A. $-3$
- B. $3$
- C. $-4$
- D. $4$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

Move the decimal point to get a number between 1 and 10: $0.0078 \to 7.8$, which is 3 places to the **right**. Moving right means dividing by $10^3$:

$$
0.0078 = 7.8 \times 10^{-3}
$$

So $n = -3$.

**Answer: A**

A number smaller than 1 always has a negative power of 10 in standard form.

## Q17 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Given that $f(x) = x$ and $g(x) = 3x - 2$, then the composite function $gf(x)$ is

**Options**

- A. $3x^2 - 2x$
- B. $\frac{x - 2}{3}$
- C. $3x - 2$
- D. $\frac{x + 2}{3}$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

$gf(x)$ means "do $f$ first, then $g$":

$$
gf(x) = g\big(f(x)\big) = g(x) = 3x - 2
$$

Since $f$ leaves $x$ unchanged, $gf(x)$ is just $g(x)$.

**Answer: C**

Option A multiplies $f(x)$ by $g(x)$, which is not composition. Option D is the inverse $g^{-1}(x)$.
