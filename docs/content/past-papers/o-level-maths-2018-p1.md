## Q1 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

$1 - \frac{1}{3}$ of $24$ equals

**Options**

- A. $16$
- B. $9$
- C. $-8$
- D. $-7$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

"Of" comes before subtraction (BODMAS):

$$
1 - \frac{1}{3} \times 24 = 1 - 8 = -7
$$

**Answer: D**

Option A works out $\left(1 - \frac{1}{3}\right) \times 24$, which ignores the order of operations.

## Q12 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

In a party of 50 persons, 30 ate chicken and 10 ate both chicken and meat. Given that everybody ate something, then the number of persons who ate meat only is

**Options**

- A. $30$
- B. $20$
- C. $40$
- D. $10$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

Everybody ate chicken, meat or both. Those who ate chicken (including both) are 30, so the rest ate **meat only**:

$$
50 - 30 = 20
$$

(Chicken only: $30 - 10 = 20$; both: 10; meat only: 20. Total: $20 + 10 + 20 = 50$. ✓)

**Answer: B**

Option A is everyone who ate meat (meat only plus both).

## Q14 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Given a function defined as $h : x \mapsto \frac{3}{x(x - 1)}$, the domain of $h$ is

**Options**

- A. $x \in \mathbb{R}, x \ne 0, 1$
- B. $x \in \mathbb{R}, x \ne 0, 1, 3$
- C. $x \in \mathbb{R}, x \ne 3$
- D. $x \in \mathbb{R}$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

We cannot divide by zero. The denominator $x(x - 1)$ is zero when $x = 0$ or $x = 1$, so those two values are excluded:

$$
\text{domain: } x \in \mathbb{R},\ x \ne 0,\ x \ne 1
$$

**Answer: A**

The numerator 3 can never cause a problem, so $x = 3$ is allowed.

## Q36 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

In the geometric progression $\frac{1}{2}, y, \frac{8}{9}, \ldots$ the value of $y$ is

**Options**

- A. $\frac{3}{2}$
- B. $\frac{3}{4}$
- C. $\frac{3}{8}$
- D. $\frac{2}{3}$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

In a G.P. the ratio is constant: $\frac{y}{1/2} = \frac{8/9}{y}$, so

$$

$$

Check: the common ratio is $\frac{2/3}{1/2} = \frac{4}{3}$, and $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{4}{3} = \frac{8}{9}$. ✓

**Answer: D**

## Q37 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Given that $y$ varies inversely as the square of $x$ and that $y = 1$ when $x = -2$, then the equation connecting $y$ to $x$ is

**Options**

- C. $y = \frac{4}{x^2}$
- D. $y = \frac{4x}{1}$

$y = \frac{k}{x^2}$. Substitute $y = 1$, $x = -2$:

$$

$$

So $y = \frac{4}{x^2}$.

**Answer: C**

Option A is direct variation with $x^2$.

## Q46 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

A transformation matrix is $M$ where $M = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$. $M$ is described as

**Options**

- A. A stretch of 2 units along the $x$-axis
- B. A shear of 2 units parallel to the $y$-axis
- C. A shear of 2 units parallel to the $x$-axis
- D. A stretch of 2 units along the $y$-axis

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

$M$ sends $(x, y)$ to $(x + 2y,\ y)$: every point keeps its height $y$ and slides sideways by $2y$. Moving parallel to the $x$-axis by an amount proportional to $y$ is a **shear parallel to the $x$-axis** with factor 2.

**Answer: C**

A stretch along the $x$-axis would be $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$.

## Q50 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Two mutually exclusive events $M$ and $N$ are such that $P(M) = \frac{1}{3}$ and $P(N) = \frac{1}{2}$, then $P(M \cup N) =$

**Options**

- A. $0$
- B. $\frac{2}{5}$
- C. $\frac{2}{3}$
- D. $\frac{5}{6}$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

Mutually exclusive events cannot happen together, so their probabilities add:

$$
P(M \cup N) = \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{2}{6} + \frac{3}{6} = \frac{5}{6}
$$

**Answer: D**

Option A is $P(M \cap N)$, which is 0 for mutually exclusive events.
