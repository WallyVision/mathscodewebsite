## Q1 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Evaluating $4 - 8 \div 4 + 8 =$

**Options**

- A. $1$
- B. $6$
- C. $7$
- D. $10$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

Division first, then add and subtract from left to right:

$$
4 - 8 \div 4 + 8 = 4 - 2 + 8 = 10
$$

**Answer: D**

Working from left to right without BODMAS gives $(4 - 8) \div 4 + 8 = 7$, option C.

**Options**

## Q7 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Given the sets of numbers $\mathbb{N}$, $\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{R}$, the true statement is

**Options**

- A. $\sqrt{7} \in \mathbb{Q}$
- B. $-4 \notin \mathbb{Z}$
- C. $\mathbb{N} \subset \mathbb{R}$
- D. $\mathbb{Q} \subset \mathbb{N}$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

Every natural number is a real number, so $\mathbb{N} \subset \mathbb{R}$.

- A is false: $\sqrt{7}$ is irrational.
- B is false: $-4$ is an integer.
- D is false: $\frac{1}{2} \in \mathbb{Q}$ but not in $\mathbb{N}$.

**Answer: C**

**Options**

Given two sets $P$ and $L$ defined as

## Q18 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

A function $h : x \mapsto \frac{3x - 5}{7}$, where $x \in \mathbb{R}$. The inverse $h^{-1}(x)$ is

**Options**

- A. $\frac{7x - 5}{3}$
- B. $\frac{7x + 5}{3}$
- C. $\frac{7x - 3}{5}$
- D. $\frac{3x + 5}{7}$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

Let $y = \frac{3x - 5}{7}$ and make $x$ the subject:

$$
7y = 3x - 5 \quad\Rightarrow\quad 3x = 7y + 5 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x = \frac{7y + 5}{3}
$$

So $h^{-1}(x) = \frac{7x + 5}{3}$.

Check: $h(5) = \frac{10}{7}$ and $h^{-1}\!\left(\frac{10}{7}\right) = \frac{10 + 5}{3} = 5$. ✓

**Answer: B**

Option A forgets to change "$-5$" to "$+5$" when it moves across.

The expression $\frac{1}{x - 1} - \frac{3}{1 - x}$ is equal to

## Q38 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

The sum of the first $n$ terms in a sequence is given by $S_n = 2n(n - 1)$. The second term is

**Options**

- A. $8$
- B. $0$
- C. $4$
- D. $2$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

The second term is the sum of two terms minus the first term:

$$
S_2 = 2(2)(1) = 4, \qquad S_1 = 2(1)(0) = 0
$$

$$
u_2 = S_2 - S_1 = 4 - 0 = 4
$$

**Answer: C**
