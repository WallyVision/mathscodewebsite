## Q1 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

$\frac{a^m}{a^n} =$

**Options**

- A. $a^{\frac{m}{n}}$
- B. $a^{m + n}$
- C. $a^{m - n}$
- D. $a^{mn}$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

When dividing powers of the same base, subtract the indices: $\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m - n}$.

**Answer: C**

B is for multiplying, and D is for $(a^m)^n$.

## Q2 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

$\log_3 81 =$

**Options**

- A. $27$
- B. $9$
- C. $4$
- D. $3$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

$3^4 = 81$, so $\log_3 81 = 4$.

**Answer: C**

Option A is $81 \div 3$, and B is $\sqrt{81}$.

## Q3 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

The conjugate of $2 + \sqrt{3}$ is

**Options**

- A. $2 - \sqrt{3}$
- B. $2 + \sqrt{3}$
- C. $3 - \sqrt{2}$
- D. $3 + \sqrt{2}$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

The conjugate keeps the whole-number part and changes the sign of the surd: $2 - \sqrt{3}$. Their product $4 - 3 = 1$ has no surd.

**Answer: A**

Option B is the number itself.

## Q4 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

The sum of the roots of the quadratic equation $2x^2 - 3x + 1 = 0$ is

**Options**

- A. $-\frac{3}{2}$
- B. $\frac{1}{2}$
- C. $\frac{2}{3}$
- D. $\frac{3}{2}$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

$$
\alpha + \beta = -\frac{b}{a} = -\frac{-3}{2} = \frac{3}{2}
$$

(The roots are 1 and $\frac{1}{2}$: $1 + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2}$. ✓)

**Answer: D**

Option A forgets the minus sign in $-\frac{b}{a}$. Option B is the product of the roots.

## Q5 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Given that the sum of the roots of the equation $2x^2 - kx + 18 = 0$ is $-3$, the value of $k$ is

**Options**

- A. $-6$
- B. $-\frac{1}{6}$
- C. $\frac{1}{6}$
- D. $6$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

$$
\alpha + \beta = -\frac{b}{a} = -\frac{-k}{2} = \frac{k}{2} = -3 \quad\Rightarrow\quad k = -6
$$

**Answer: A**

Option D comes from losing a minus sign.
