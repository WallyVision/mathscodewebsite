## Q1 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

$a^{\frac{1}{2}} =$

**Options**

- A. $\sqrt{a}$
- B. $\frac{1}{a}$
- C. $\sqrt{2a}$
- D. $\frac{1}{a^2}$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

A power of $\frac{1}{2}$ means the square root: $a^{1/2} = \sqrt{a}$.

**Answer: A**

B is $a^{-1}$ and D is $a^{-2}$: negative powers give reciprocals, fractional powers give roots.

- B. $(\alpha + \beta)^2 + 2\alpha\beta$

**Answer: C**
