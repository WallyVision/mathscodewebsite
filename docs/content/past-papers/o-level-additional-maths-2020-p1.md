## Q1 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

$a^m \times a^n =$

**Options**

- A. $a^{m - n}$
- B. $a^{m + n}$
- C. $(a^m)^n$
- D. $a^m + a^n$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

When multiplying powers of the same base, add the indices: $a^m \times a^n = a^{m + n}$.

**Answer: B**

A is for division, and C equals $a^{mn}$.

- C. $-\frac{1}{3}\cos 3x + k$
- D. $-3\cos 3x + k$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

$\int \sin(ax)\,dx = -\frac{1}{a}\cos(ax) + k$. With $a = 3$:

$$
\int \sin 3x\,dx = -\frac{1}{3}\cos 3x + k
$$

**Answer: C**

Option D multiplies by 3 instead of dividing.

## Q50 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

The area of the region bounded by the curve $y = x^2$ and the $x$-axis in the range $0 \le x \le 2$ is

**Options**

- A. $\frac{8}{3}$
- B. $2$
- C. $\frac{4}{3}$
- D. $\frac{3}{8}$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

$$
\int_0^2 x^2\,dx = \left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^2 = \frac{8}{3}
$$

**Answer: A**

Option D is the answer upside down.
