## Q1 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

$\frac{1}{a^n}$ is the same as

**Options**

- A. $a^n$
- B. $a^{-n}$
- C. $\sqrt[n]{a}$
- D. $a^{-\frac{1}{n}}$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

A negative index means a reciprocal: $a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}$.

**Answer: B**

Option C is $a^{1/n}$: fractional indices give roots, negative ones give reciprocals.

## Q2 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Given that $\log_q p = r$, then

**Options**

- A. $p = q^r$
- B. $q = p^r$
- C. $p = rq$
- D. $q = rp$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

$\log_q p = r$ means "$q$ to the power $r$ gives $p$": $p = q^r$. (For example $\log_2 8 = 3$ because $2^3 = 8$.)

**Answer: A**

Option B swaps the base and the number.

## Q30 · difficulty 3

**Stem**

A region is drawn on the $xy$-plane. It is bounded by the $y$-axis (drawn as a solid line) and by the **broken** lines $y = 2x$ and $2x + y = 4$. The shaded region lies to the right of the $y$-axis, above $y = 2x$ and below $2x + y = 4$: the triangle with corners $(0, 0)$, $(1, 2)$ and $(0, 4)$.

The set of inequalities that satisfies the shaded region is

**Options**

- A. $\{x \ge 0,\ y \ge 2x,\ 2x + y \le 4\}$
- B. $\{x \ge 0,\ y > 2x,\ 2x + y < 4\}$
- C. $\{x \ge 0,\ y \le 2x,\ 2x + y \le 4\}$
- D. $\{x \ge 0,\ y < 2x,\ 2x + y < 4\}$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

- Right of the $y$-axis, which is solid: $x \ge 0$.
- Above $y = 2x$: test $(0, 2)$, where $2 > 0$, so $y > 2x$.
- Below $2x + y = 4$: test $(0, 2)$, where $2 < 4$, so $2x + y < 4$.

A **broken** line means the boundary is not included, so those two signs are strict ($>$ and $<$).

**Answer: B**

Option A has the right sides but uses $\ge$ and $\le$, which would need solid lines. C and D are on the wrong side of $y = 2x$.

- A. $6x$
- B. $3x$
- C. $x^2$
- D. $3x^2$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

$3 \times 2x = 6x$.

**Answer: A**

## Q46 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

$\frac{d}{dx}(\cos 2x) =$

**Options**

- A. $\sin 2x$
- B. $-\sin 2x$
- C. $2\sin 2x$
- D. $-2\sin 2x$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

By the chain rule: $-\sin(2x) \times 2 = -2\sin 2x$.

**Answer: D**

Option B forgets the factor 2, and C has the wrong sign.

## Q47 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

The value of $x$ at which the function $y = 5 + 2x - x^2$ has its maximum value is

**Options**

- A. $1$
- B. $2$
- C. $4$
- D. $6$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

$\frac{dy}{dx} = 2 - 2x = 0$ gives $x = 1$, and $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = -2 < 0$ confirms a maximum.

**Answer: A**

Option D is the maximum **value**, $5 + 2 - 1 = 6$, not where it occurs.

## Q48 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

$\displaystyle\int x^3\,dx =$ (where $k$ is an arbitrary constant of integration)

**Options**

- A. $3x^2 + k$
- B. $\frac{x^3}{3} + k$
- C. $\frac{x^4}{4} + k$
- D. $x^4 + k$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

Raise the power by 1 and divide by the new power: $\frac{x^4}{4} + k$.

**Answer: C**

Option A differentiates instead.

## Q49 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

$\displaystyle\int \sin 2x\,dx =$ (where $k$ is an arbitrary constant of integration)

**Options**

- A. $2\cos 2x + k$
- B. $-\frac{1}{2}\cos 2x + k$
- C. $\frac{1}{2}\sin 2x + k$
- D. $-2\sin 2x + k$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

$\int \sin(ax)\,dx = -\frac{1}{a}\cos(ax) + k$, so the answer is $-\frac{1}{2}\cos 2x + k$.

Check: differentiating gives $-\frac{1}{2} \times (-2\sin 2x) = \sin 2x$. ✓

**Answer: B**

## Q50 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

The area bounded by the curve $y = x^2$ and the $x$-axis in the range $0 \le x \le 2$ is

**Options**

- A. $4$
- B. $\frac{4}{8}$
- C. $\frac{3}{8}$
- D. $\frac{8}{3}$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

$$\int_0^2 x^2\,dx = \left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^2 = \frac{8}{3}$$

**Answer: D**

Option A is the value of $y$ at $x = 2$, and C is the answer upside down.
