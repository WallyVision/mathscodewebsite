## Q1 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Simplifying $\frac{1}{3}$ of $(-3 + 12)$ gives

**Options**

- A. $-5$
- B. $3$
- C. $5$
- D. $-5$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

Work out the bracket first, then "of" means multiply:

$$
\frac{1}{3} \text{ of } (-3 + 12) = \frac{1}{3} \times 9 = 3
$$

**Answer: B**

## Q2 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

The arithmetic property for which $5 + 3 = 3 + 5$ is

**Options**

- A. Associative
- B. Distributive
- C. Commutative
- D. Additive

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

Changing the **order** of the numbers does not change the sum: $a + b = b + a$. This is the commutative property.

**Answer: C**

The associative property is about grouping, $(a + b) + c = a + (b + c)$, and the distributive property is $a(b + c) = ab + ac$.

## Q4 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

The H.C.F of $2a^2b$ and $ab$ is

**Options**

- A. $a^2b$
- B. $2a^2b$
- C. $ab$
- D. $2a^3b^2$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

Take what both terms share, with the lower power: the number 2 is only in the first term, $a$ appears as $a^2$ and $a$ (take $a$), and $b$ appears in both (take $b$).

$$
\text{H.C.F} = ab
$$

**Answer: C**

Option B is the L.C.M.

## Q6 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Given that a dress is sold for 6000 FCFA and a profit of 20% made on it. The cost price in FCFA is

**Options**

- A. 5000
- B. 4800
- C. 7200
- D. 12000

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

The profit is 20% of the cost price, so the selling price is 120% of the cost price:

$$
1.2 \times \text{cost} = 6000 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \text{cost} = \frac{6000}{1.2} = 5000 \text{ FCFA}
$$

Check: 20% of 5000 is 1000, and $5000 + 1000 = 6000$. ✓

**Answer: A**

Option B takes 20% off the selling price, which is the common mistake.

## Q20 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

An interior angle of a regular plane figure is $90^\circ$, the number of sides is

**Options**

- A. $6$
- B. $3$
- C. $4$
- D. $5$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

Each exterior angle is $180^\circ - 90^\circ = 90^\circ$. The exterior angles add up to $360^\circ$, so

$$
\text{number of sides} = \frac{360^\circ}{90^\circ} = 4
$$

(It is a square.)

**Answer: C**

## Q37 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

The trigonometry ratio which is positive in the fourth quadrant is

**Options**

- A. Cosine
- B. Tangent
- C. Sine
- D. Cosecant

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

In the fourth quadrant ($270^\circ$ to $360^\circ$), $x$ is positive and $y$ is negative. Cosine follows $x$, so it is positive. Sine (and so cosecant) and tangent are negative there.

Memory aid "**A**ll **S**tudents **T**ake **C**offee": All positive in the 1st quadrant, Sine in the 2nd, Tangent in the 3rd, Cosine in the 4th.

**Answer: A**

## Q50 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

The probability of selecting a vowel from the word GOVERNANCE is
