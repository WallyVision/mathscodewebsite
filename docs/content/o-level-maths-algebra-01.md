## Q1 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Simplify $3a + 5b - a + 2b$.

**Options**

- A. $4a + 7b$
- B. $2a + 3b$
- C. $2a + 7b$
- D. $9ab$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

Collect like terms.

$$
3a - a = 2a, \qquad 5b + 2b = 7b
$$

So $3a + 5b - a + 2b = 2a + 7b$. **Answer: C**

Option A adds $a$ instead of subtracting it. Option D wrongly combines unlike terms: $a$ and $b$ terms cannot be added together.

## Q2 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Expand $3(2x - 5)$.

**Options**

- A. $6x - 15$
- B. $6x - 5$
- C. $5x - 15$
- D. $6x + 15$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

Multiply **every** term in the bracket by 3.

$$
3 \times 2x = 6x, \qquad 3 \times (-5) = -15
$$

So $3(2x - 5) = 6x - 15$. **Answer: A**

Option B multiplies only the first term. Option D loses the minus sign.

## Q3 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Expand and simplify $(x + 3)(x - 5)$.

**Options**

- A. $x^2 + 2x - 15$
- B. $x^2 - 2x - 15$
- C. $x^2 - 15$
- D. $x^2 - 8x - 15$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

Multiply each term in the first bracket by each term in the second.

$$
(x + 3)(x - 5) = x^2 - 5x + 3x - 15 = x^2 - 2x - 15
$$

**Answer: B**

Option C forgets the middle terms. Option A gets the sign of $-5x + 3x$ wrong.

## Q4 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Find the value of $2x^2 - 3y$ when $x = -3$ and $y = 4$.

**Options**

- A. $-30$
- B. $24$
- C. $30$
- D. $6$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

Substitute, squaring first: $(-3)^2 = 9$.

$$
2x^2 - 3y = 2(9) - 3(4) = 18 - 12 = 6
$$

**Answer: D**

Option A treats $(-3)^2$ as $-9$. Option B squares $2x$ instead of $x$: $(2 \times -3)^2 = 36$.

## Q5 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Solve $5x + 3 = 2x - 9$.

**Options**

- A. $x = -4$
- B. $x = 4$
- C. $x = -2$
- D. $x = -\dfrac{12}{7}$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

Put the $x$ terms on one side and the numbers on the other.

$$
\begin{aligned}
5x - 2x &= -9 - 3 \\
3x &= -12 \\
x &= -4
\end{aligned}
$$

Check: $5(-4) + 3 = -17$ and $2(-4) - 9 = -17$. **Answer: A**

Option C comes from $-9 + 3 = -6$ (wrong sign when moving the 3). Option D adds $2x$ instead of subtracting it.

## Q7 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Factorise completely $6x^2 - 9x$.

**Options**

- A. $3(2x^2 - 3x)$
- B. $3x(2x - 3)$
- C. $x(6x - 9)$
- D. $3x(2x - 9)$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

The highest common factor of $6x^2$ and $9x$ is $3x$.

$$
6x^2 - 9x = 3x(2x - 3)
$$

Check by expanding: $3x \times 2x = 6x^2$, $3x \times (-3) = -9x$. **Answer: B**

Options A and C are correct but **not complete**: a common factor is still left inside the bracket.

## Q8 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Factorise $x^2 - 7x + 12$.

**Options**

- A. $(x + 3)(x + 4)$
- B. $(x - 2)(x - 6)$
- C. $(x - 3)(x - 4)$
- D. $(x + 3)(x - 4)$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

Find two numbers that **multiply** to $+12$ and **add** to $-7$: they are $-3$ and $-4$.

$$
x^2 - 7x + 12 = (x - 3)(x - 4)
$$

**Answer: C**

Option A gives $+7x$. Option B multiplies to 12 but adds to $-8$. Option D gives $x^2 - x - 12$.

## Q9 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Factorise $4x^2 - 25$.

**Options**

- A. $(2x - 5)^2$
- B. $(4x - 5)(4x + 5)$
- C. $(2x + 5)^2$
- D. $(2x - 5)(2x + 5)$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

This is a difference of two squares, $a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$, with $a = 2x$ and $b = 5$.

$$
4x^2 - 25 = (2x)^2 - 5^2 = (2x - 5)(2x + 5)
$$

**Answer: D**

Option A expands to $4x^2 - 20x + 25$. Option B expands to $16x^2 - 25$.

## Q10 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

The sum of three consecutive whole numbers is 72. What is the smallest of the three numbers?

**Options**

- A. 22
- B. 24
- C. 25
- D. 23

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

Let the numbers be $n$, $n + 1$ and $n + 2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
n + (n + 1) + (n + 2) &= 72 \\
3n + 3 &= 72 \\
n &= 23
\end{aligned}
$$

The numbers are 23, 24 and 25. **Answer: D**

Option B is the middle number ($72 \div 3$) and option C is the largest: read the question carefully.

## Q11 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Solve the simultaneous equations

$$
2x + 3y = 12, \qquad x - y = 1
$$

**Options**

- A. $x = 3,\ y = 2$
- B. $x = 2,\ y = 3$
- C. $x = 3.8,\ y = 2.8$
- D. $x = 6,\ y = 0$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

From the second equation, $x = y + 1$. Substitute into the first:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2(y + 1) + 3y &= 12 \\
5y + 2 &= 12 \\
y &= 2
\end{aligned}
$$

Then $x = 2 + 1 = 3$. Check: $2(3) + 3(2) = 12$ and $3 - 2 = 1$. **Answer: A**

Option B swaps $x$ and $y$. Option C comes from expanding $2(y + 1)$ as $2y - 2$. Option D fits the first equation but not the second.

## Q12 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Solve $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$.

**Options**

- A. $x = -2$ or $x = -3$
- B. $x = 2$ or $x = 3$
- C. $x = 1$ or $x = 6$
- D. $x = -1$ or $x = 6$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

Factorise: two numbers that multiply to $6$ and add to $-5$ are $-2$ and $-3$.

$$
(x - 2)(x - 3) = 0 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x = 2 \ \text{or}\ x = 3
$$

**Answer: B**

Option A uses the numbers from the brackets without changing their sign. Option D solves $x^2 - 5x - 6 = 0$.

## Q13 · difficulty 3

**Stem**

Solve $2x^2 + 3x - 2 = 0$.

**Options**

- A. $x = 1$ or $x = -2$
- B. $x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ or $x = 2$
- C. $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ or $x = 2$
- D. $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ or $x = -2$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

Multiply $a \times c = 2 \times (-2) = -4$. Two numbers that multiply to $-4$ and add to $3$ are $4$ and $-1$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x^2 + 4x - x - 2 &= 0 \\
2x(x + 2) - (x + 2) &= 0 \\
(2x - 1)(x + 2) &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

So $2x - 1 = 0$, giving $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$, or $x + 2 = 0$, giving $x = -2$. **Answer: D**

Option A forgets to divide by 2 when solving $2x - 1 = 0$. Option B has both signs reversed.

## Q14 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Make $r$ the subject of the formula $A = \pi r^2$.

**Options**

- A. $r = \dfrac{A}{2\pi}$
- B. $r = \sqrt{\dfrac{A}{\pi}}$
- C. $r = \dfrac{\sqrt{A}}{\pi}$
- D. $r = \sqrt{A\pi}$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

Divide both sides by $\pi$, then take the square root.

$$
r^2 = \frac{A}{\pi} \quad\Rightarrow\quad r = \sqrt{\frac{A}{\pi}}
$$

($r$ is a radius, so we take the positive root.) **Answer: B**

Option A halves instead of taking the square root. Option C takes the root of $A$ only, not of $\pi$ as well.

## Q15 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Make $x$ the subject of $y = \dfrac{3x - 2}{5}$.

**Options**

- A. $x = \dfrac{5y - 2}{3}$
- B. $x = \dfrac{y + 2}{15}$
- C. $x = \dfrac{5y + 2}{3}$
- D. $x = \dfrac{3y + 2}{5}$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

Undo each step in reverse order: multiply by 5, add 2, divide by 3.

$$
\begin{aligned}
5y &= 3x - 2 \\
5y + 2 &= 3x \\
x &= \frac{5y + 2}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Answer: C**

Option A subtracts 2 instead of adding it. Option D swaps the 3 and the 5.

## Q16 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Solve the inequality $3x - 4 > 11$.

**Options**

- A. $x > 5$
- B. $x > \dfrac{7}{3}$
- C. $x < 5$
- D. $x > 15$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

Add 4 to both sides, then divide by 3 (a positive number, so the sign stays the same).

$$
3x > 15 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x > 5
$$

**Answer: A**

Option B subtracts 4 instead of adding it. Option C reverses the sign, which is only needed when dividing by a negative number.

## Q18 · difficulty 3

**Stem**

Simplify $\dfrac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 + 3x}$.

**Options**

- A. $-\dfrac{3}{x}$
- B. $\dfrac{x - 3}{x}$
- C. $\dfrac{x + 3}{x}$
- D. $x - 3$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

Factorise the top and the bottom, then cancel the common factor $(x + 3)$.

$$
\frac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 + 3x} = \frac{(x - 3)(x + 3)}{x(x + 3)} = \frac{x - 3}{x}
$$

**Answer: B**

Option A cancels the $x^2$ terms, which is not allowed: you can only cancel **factors**, never terms that are added or subtracted.

## Q19 · difficulty 3

**Stem**

Express $\dfrac{2}{x} + \dfrac{3}{x + 1}$ as a single fraction.

**Options**

- A. $\dfrac{5x + 1}{x(x + 1)}$
- B. $\dfrac{5}{x(x + 1)}$
- C. $\dfrac{5x + 2}{x(x + 1)}$
- D. $\dfrac{5}{2x + 1}$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

The common denominator is $x(x + 1)$.

$$
\frac{2}{x} + \frac{3}{x + 1} = \frac{2(x + 1) + 3x}{x(x + 1)} = \frac{2x + 2 + 3x}{x(x + 1)} = \frac{5x + 2}{x(x + 1)}
$$

**Answer: C**

Option A expands $2(x + 1)$ as $2x + 1$. Option D adds the tops and the bottoms separately, which is never correct for fractions.

## Q20 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

$y$ varies inversely as $x$, and $y = 6$ when $x = 4$. Find $y$ when $x = 3$.

**Options**

- A. $8$
- B. $4.5$
- C. $18$
- D. $2$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

Inverse variation means $y = \dfrac{k}{x}$.

$$
6 = \frac{k}{4} \quad\Rightarrow\quad k = 24
$$

When $x = 3$: $y = \dfrac{24}{3} = 8$. **Answer: A**

Option B uses **direct** variation ($y = 1.5x$). Check: as $x$ gets smaller, an inversely varying $y$ must get **bigger**, so B and D cannot be right.
