## Q1 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

In triangle $ABC$, angle $B = 90^\circ$, $AB = 3\,\text{cm}$ and $BC = 4\,\text{cm}$. Find the length of $AC$.

**Options**

- A. $7\,\text{cm}$
- B. $5\,\text{cm}$
- C. $25\,\text{cm}$
- D. $\sqrt{7}\,\text{cm}$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

$AC$ is opposite the right angle, so it is the hypotenuse. By Pythagoras' theorem:

$$
AC^2 = AB^2 + BC^2 = 3^2 + 4^2 = 9 + 16 = 25 \quad\Rightarrow\quad AC = 5\,\text{cm}
$$

**Answer: B**

Option A adds the sides without squaring. Option C forgets to take the square root.

## Q2 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

In triangle $PQR$, angle $Q = 90^\circ$, $PQ = 5\,\text{cm}$ and $PR = 13\,\text{cm}$. Find $QR$.

**Options**

- A. $12\,\text{cm}$
- B. $\sqrt{194}\,\text{cm}$
- C. $8\,\text{cm}$
- D. $18\,\text{cm}$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

$PR$ is the hypotenuse (opposite the right angle), so **subtract**:

$$
QR^2 = PR^2 - PQ^2 = 169 - 25 = 144 \quad\Rightarrow\quad QR = 12\,\text{cm}
$$

**Answer: A**

Option B adds the squares, treating $QR$ as the hypotenuse. A shorter side can never be longer than the hypotenuse, so B (about $13.9\,\text{cm}$) is impossible.

## Q3 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

In a right-angled triangle, the side opposite angle $\theta$ is $6\,\text{cm}$ and the hypotenuse is $10\,\text{cm}$. Find $\sin\theta$.

**Options**

- A. $0.8$
- B. $0.75$
- C. $0.6$
- D. $1.67$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

$$
\sin\theta = \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}} = \frac{6}{10} = 0.6
$$

**Answer: C**

Option A is $\cos\theta$ (the adjacent side is $8\,\text{cm}$). Option D turns the ratio upside down: a sine can never be greater than 1.

## Q5 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

What is the exact value of $\cos 60^\circ$?

**Options**

- A. $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- B. $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- C. $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$
- D. $\sqrt{3}$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

Learn the special angles:

|        | $30^\circ$            | $45^\circ$            | $60^\circ$            |
| ------ | --------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------- |
| $\sin$ | $\tfrac{1}{2}$        | $\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ | $\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ |
| $\cos$ | $\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ | $\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ | $\tfrac{1}{2}$        |
| $\tan$ | $\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$ | $1$                   | $\sqrt{3}$            |

So $\cos 60^\circ = \dfrac{1}{2}$. **Answer: B**

Option A is $\cos 30^\circ$ (or $\sin 60^\circ$). Option D is $\tan 60^\circ$.

## Q6 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Find the exact value of $\sin 45^\circ \times \cos 45^\circ$.

**Options**

- A. $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- B. $1$
- C. $\sqrt{2}$
- D. $\dfrac{1}{4}$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

$$
\sin 45^\circ \times \cos 45^\circ = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \times \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{1}{2}
$$

**Answer: A**

Option B is $\tan 45^\circ$, or the result of adding the two squares ($\sin^2 + \cos^2 = 1$) instead of multiplying.

## Q7 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

A ladder $10\,\text{m}$ long leans against a vertical wall. The ladder makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the level ground. How high up the wall does the ladder reach?

**Options**

- A. $5\,\text{m}$
- B. $10\sqrt{3}\,\text{m}$
- C. $20\,\text{m}$
- D. $5\sqrt{3}\,\text{m}$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

The ladder is the hypotenuse. The height up the wall is **opposite** the $60^\circ$ angle at the ground.

$$
h = 10\sin 60^\circ = 10 \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 5\sqrt{3}\,\text{m} \approx 8.66\,\text{m}
$$

**Answer: D**

Option A uses $\cos 60^\circ$, which gives the distance of the foot of the ladder from the wall. Option C cannot be right: the height can never be more than the ladder's length.

## Q8 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

From a point on level ground $20\,\text{m}$ from the foot of a tree, the angle of elevation of the top of the tree is $45^\circ$. How tall is the tree?

**Options**

- A. $20\sqrt{2}\,\text{m}$
- B. $10\,\text{m}$
- C. $20\,\text{m}$
- D. $10\sqrt{2}\,\text{m}$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

The height is opposite the $45^\circ$ angle and the $20\,\text{m}$ is adjacent to it.

$$
\tan 45^\circ = \frac{h}{20} \quad\Rightarrow\quad h = 20 \times 1 = 20\,\text{m}
$$

**Answer: C**

Option A is the distance from the point to the **top** of the tree (the hypotenuse).

## Q9 · difficulty 3

**Stem**

From the top of a vertical cliff $30\,\text{m}$ high, the angle of depression of a boat at sea is $30^\circ$. How far is the boat from the foot of the cliff?

**Options**

- A. $15\,\text{m}$
- B. $30\sqrt{3}\,\text{m}$
- C. $10\sqrt{3}\,\text{m}$
- D. $60\,\text{m}$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

The angle of depression from the cliff top equals the angle of elevation from the boat (alternate angles), so the angle at the boat is $30^\circ$. The cliff ($30\,\text{m}$) is opposite this angle and the distance $d$ is adjacent.

$$
\tan 30^\circ = \frac{30}{d} \quad\Rightarrow\quad d = \frac{30}{\tan 30^\circ} = \frac{30}{1/\sqrt{3}} = 30\sqrt{3}\,\text{m} \approx 52\,\text{m}
$$

**Answer: B**

Option C multiplies by $\tan 30^\circ$ instead of dividing. Option D is the straight-line distance from the cliff top to the boat ($30 \div \sin 30^\circ$), not the distance along the sea.

## Q10 · difficulty 3

**Stem**

In triangle $ABC$, angle $A = 30^\circ$, angle $B = 45^\circ$ and $BC = 10\,\text{cm}$. Find $AC$.

**Options**

- A. $10\sqrt{2}\,\text{cm}$
- B. $5\sqrt{2}\,\text{cm}$
- C. $20\,\text{cm}$
- D. $15\,\text{cm}$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

$BC$ is opposite angle $A$ and $AC$ is opposite angle $B$. Use the sine rule:

$$
\frac{AC}{\sin B} = \frac{BC}{\sin A} \quad\Rightarrow\quad AC = \frac{10 \sin 45^\circ}{\sin 30^\circ} = \frac{10 \times \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}}{\frac{1}{2}} = \frac{20}{\sqrt{2}} = 10\sqrt{2}\,\text{cm}
$$

($\approx 14.1\,\text{cm}$.) **Answer: A**

Option B swaps the sines: $\dfrac{10 \sin 30^\circ}{\sin 45^\circ}$. Always pair each side with the angle **opposite** it.

## Q11 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

In triangle $ABC$, $AB = 8\,\text{cm}$, $AC = 5\,\text{cm}$ and angle $A = 60^\circ$. Find $BC$.

**Options**

- A. $\sqrt{89}\,\text{cm}$
- B. $7\,\text{cm}$
- C. $49\,\text{cm}$
- D. $\sqrt{129}\,\text{cm}$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

Two sides and the angle between them are known, so use the cosine rule:

$$
\begin{aligned}
BC^2 &= AB^2 + AC^2 - 2(AB)(AC)\cos A \\
&= 64 + 25 - 2(8)(5)\left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right) \\
&= 89 - 40 = 49
\end{aligned}
$$

So $BC = 7\,\text{cm}$. **Answer: B**

Option A forgets the $-2bc\cos A$ term (that is Pythagoras, only for $90^\circ$). Option C forgets the square root. Option D adds the term instead of subtracting it.

## Q12 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Two sides of a triangle are $6\,\text{cm}$ and $8\,\text{cm}$, and the angle between them is $30^\circ$. Find the area of the triangle.

**Options**

- A. $24\,\text{cm}^2$
- B. $48\,\text{cm}^2$
- C. $12\,\text{cm}^2$
- D. $20.8\,\text{cm}^2$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

$$
\text{Area} = \tfrac{1}{2}ab\sin C = \tfrac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 8 \times \sin 30^\circ = 24 \times \tfrac{1}{2} = 12\,\text{cm}^2
$$

**Answer: C**

Option A leaves out $\sin C$ (which treats the angle as $90^\circ$). Option D uses $\cos 30^\circ$ instead of $\sin 30^\circ$.

## Q13 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Given that $\sin 40^\circ = 0.643$ and $\cos 40^\circ = 0.766$, find $\sin 140^\circ$.

**Options**

- A. $-0.643$
- B. $0.643$
- C. $0.766$
- D. $-0.766$

**Correct:** B

**Solution**

For any angle, $\sin(180^\circ - x) = \sin x$. Here $140^\circ = 180^\circ - 40^\circ$, so

$$
\sin 140^\circ = \sin 40^\circ = 0.643
$$

Sine is positive for angles between $90^\circ$ and $180^\circ$. **Answer: B**

Option A has the wrong sign: it is **cosine** that is negative in this range ($\cos 140^\circ = -0.766$, option D).

## Q14 · difficulty 3

**Stem**

Solve $\sin x = 0.5$ for $0^\circ \le x \le 360^\circ$.

**Options**

- A. $x = 30^\circ$ or $x = 330^\circ$
- B. $x = 60^\circ$ or $x = 120^\circ$
- C. $x = 30^\circ$ or $x = 210^\circ$
- D. $x = 30^\circ$ or $x = 150^\circ$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

The basic angle is $x = 30^\circ$, since $\sin 30^\circ = \tfrac{1}{2}$. Sine is positive in the first and second quadrants, so the second solution is

$$
x = 180^\circ - 30^\circ = 150^\circ
$$

**Answer: D**

Option A gives the solutions of $\cos x = \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ (cosine is positive in the first and fourth quadrants). Option B gives the solutions of $\sin x = \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

## Q15 · difficulty 1

**Stem**

Town $B$ is due east of town $A$. What is the bearing of $A$ from $B$?

**Options**

- A. $090^\circ$
- B. $180^\circ$
- C. $270^\circ$
- D. $360^\circ$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

Bearings are measured clockwise from north. Standing at $B$, town $A$ is due **west**, which is $270^\circ$. **Answer: C**

Option A is the bearing of $B$ from $A$. Read "from" carefully: it tells you where you are standing.

## Q16 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

The bearing of $Q$ from $P$ is $060^\circ$. What is the bearing of $P$ from $Q$?

**Options**

- A. $060^\circ$
- B. $120^\circ$
- C. $300^\circ$
- D. $240^\circ$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

A back bearing differs by $180^\circ$. Since $060^\circ$ is less than $180^\circ$, add:

$$
060^\circ + 180^\circ = 240^\circ
$$

**Answer: D**

Option C subtracts from $360^\circ$, which reflects the direction in the north–south line instead of reversing it.

## Q17 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Given that $\cos\theta = \dfrac{4}{5}$ and $\theta$ is acute, find $\tan\theta$.

**Options**

- A. $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- B. $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- C. $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- D. $\dfrac{5}{3}$

**Correct:** C

**Solution**

Draw a right-angled triangle with adjacent side $4$ and hypotenuse $5$. By Pythagoras, the opposite side is $\sqrt{5^2 - 4^2} = 3$.

$$
\tan\theta = \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}} = \frac{3}{4}
$$

**Answer: C**

Option A is $\sin\theta$. Option B is the ratio upside down.

## Q18 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

Simplify $1 - \cos^2\theta$.

**Options**

- A. $\sin^2\theta$
- B. $\tan^2\theta$
- C. $-\sin^2\theta$
- D. $\sin\theta$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

For every angle, $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$. Rearranging:

$$
1 - \cos^2\theta = \sin^2\theta
$$

**Answer: A**

Option D forgets that the identity involves the **squares**.

## Q19 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

In a right-angled triangle, the hypotenuse is $20\,\text{cm}$ and one of the acute angles is $35^\circ$. Find the length of the side opposite the $35^\circ$ angle. (Use $\sin 35^\circ = 0.574$, $\cos 35^\circ = 0.819$, $\tan 35^\circ = 0.700$.)

**Options**

- A. $16.4\,\text{cm}$
- B. $14.0\,\text{cm}$
- C. $34.8\,\text{cm}$
- D. $11.5\,\text{cm}$

**Correct:** D

**Solution**

Opposite and hypotenuse: use sine.

$$
\text{opposite} = 20 \sin 35^\circ = 20 \times 0.574 = 11.48 \approx 11.5\,\text{cm}
$$

**Answer: D**

Option A uses cosine, which gives the **adjacent** side. Option C divides by the sine; it is longer than the hypotenuse, which is impossible.

## Q20 · difficulty 2

**Stem**

A straight ramp rises $2\,\text{m}$ vertically over a horizontal distance of $2\sqrt{3}\,\text{m}$. What angle does the ramp make with the horizontal?

**Options**

- A. $30^\circ$
- B. $60^\circ$
- C. $45^\circ$
- D. $35^\circ$

**Correct:** A

**Solution**

The rise is opposite the angle and the horizontal distance is adjacent.

$$
\tan\theta = \frac{2}{2\sqrt{3}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \theta = 30^\circ
$$

**Answer: A**

Option B uses $\dfrac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{opposite}} = \sqrt{3}$, the ratio upside down. Option D treats $\dfrac{2}{2\sqrt{3}}$ as a sine, using the horizontal distance as if it were the hypotenuse.
